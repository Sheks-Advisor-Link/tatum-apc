# Tatum APC — repository context

This repository holds APC (SAICA Assessment of Professional Competence) preparation
material for a candidate on the **UCT APC Professional Programme**. It is not a
software project. The work here is exam preparation: case study answers, technical
research and exam technique.

## Candidate and programme context

- Candidate role in every case study: **newly qualified CA(SA)** employed at the case
  company (occasionally at the auditor instead). Never use a real name in an answer.
- Programme sequence: weekly competency development → **June mock** → **1st Qualifying
  Exam (August)** → **2nd Qualifying Exam (October)** → SAICA APC.
- Exams sat so far: June 2026 mock (Fishies Ltd), August 2026 1st QE (Diamante Ltd).
- Next: the **September / 2nd Qualifying Exam**. All work should be oriented towards it.

## How to help on this repository

**Always load `.claude/skills/apc/SKILL.md` before answering an APC task, drafting a
case study response, or giving exam technique advice.** It carries the marking
standard, the recurring marker feedback, and the writing formats. Answering from
general accounting knowledge alone produces exactly the kind of response that is
graded Limited Competence.

Non-negotiables when drafting any APC answer:

1. **Answer every component of the task**, in the requested format, to the named audience.
2. **Apply the information on the day.** Pre-prepared technical content that is not
   applied to the given facts earns nothing.
3. **Say why.** Every point carries a *because*. Breadth (what) plus depth (why).
4. **Respect the scope.** If the task says "high level", "no calculations", "ignore tax",
   or "only critique what is provided", obey it exactly.
5. **Conclude.** An analysis without a recommendation is an incomplete task.
6. No real names, no dumped extracts of standards or legislation, no contradictions
   between tasks.

## South African context

Answers apply South African law and standards: IFRS Accounting Standards, the
Income Tax Act 58 of 1962, VAT Act 89 of 1991, Companies Act 71 of 2008, King V,
the SAICA Code of Professional Conduct, ISAs, JSE Listings Requirements, and
sector legislation as relevant.
