## UCT programme 2026

**June mock — Fishies Ltd** (listed fishing, aquaculture and FMCG subsidiary of FireRock)

| Task | Subject | What it required |
|---|---|---|
| (a) | FR — onerous contracts (IAS 37) | Review an assessment: challenge the order of onerous-contract versus impairment testing, the use of relevant costing principles for a financial reporting question, and the measurement of the liability (lower of the expected loss from fulfilling and the exit penalty). Correct the calculations, do not just agree or disagree. |
| (b) | Tax — fishing fleet life cycle | s12C(1)(g) ships; s11(d) repairs versus s11(a) maintenance versus improvements; recoupment only to the extent proceeds exceed tax value; s11(o); deferral of recoupments and capital gains on replacement assets. |
| (c) | Governance and ethics | Five distinct ethical issues, strongly triggered in the pre-release; identify the trigger, explain the implication against a source of governance, and flag the tone at the top. |
| (d) | MDMC — abalone allocation | Contribution per limiting factor across dried, auction and canned options; account for Japanese supply to the same market; shipping as a step-fixed cost. |
| (e) | FR — headline earnings | Apply the JSE Circular: which remeasurements are excluded (capital platform) versus included (trading). Identify items to check, and respond to the "HEPS looks like it's improving" comment. |
| (f) | FR/FM — purchase price allocation | Challenge a reported bargain purchase gain; identify double counting, inclusion of existing goodwill, incomplete fair value work; then three transaction-specific matters to communicate to the market. |
| (g) | Internal audit — bycatch compliance | Weaknesses in a verbal, manual process; practical vessel-realistic controls; distinguish Yellow, Orange and Red code requirements; independent verification separate from the person performing the task. |

Cohort result: 19% pass rate (1% HC, 9% C, 8% BC/C). Four C's out of seven was generally
sufficient; four C's with three LC's went to borderline review.

## Patterns worth knowing

- **Seven to nine tasks.** Task letters do not always align to document letters, and one
  document can generate two tasks.
- **Financial reporting appears at least twice**, almost always as task (a) and/or (b),
  and is the strongest single indicator of overall competence.
- **Ethics is almost always present**, usually as the last task or woven into a
  governance task, and is almost never triggered in the pre-release.
- **Reviewing someone else's work** — journals, a calculation, a valuation, notes, a
  reconciliation, and now AI output — is the most common task shape in the paper.
- **A task late in the paper is often still cognitively demanding.** Pace accordingly.
- Recent papers increasingly test **integrated thinking**: a task that needs financial
  reporting plus valuation plus commercial judgement, or tax plus company law plus
  governance.
