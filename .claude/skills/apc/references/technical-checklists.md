# Technical prompts by discipline

Use these to force breadth when unpacking a trigger, and again when checking an answer.
They are prompts, not content to reproduce.

## Financial reporting

For any transaction: **initial recognition → subsequent measurement → derecognition →
presentation → disclosure**, plus deferred tax and the effect on ratios and covenants.

- Does an asset or liability exist? Control, past event, probable economic benefits,
  reliable measurement.
- Which standard, and does another apply first? (IFRS 5 before measurement; IFRS 3 before
  IFRS 11; IFRS 15 before IAS 21.)
- Point in time or over time? Whose perspective — lessee/lessor, buyer/seller,
  investor/investee, parent/group/separate?
- Judgements and estimates: what is uncertain, what did management assume, is it
  reasonable, what would change the answer?
- Events after the reporting period — adjusting or non-adjusting?
- Comparatives: re-presented, restated, or untouched?

Standards that recur in APC cases: IFRS 2 share-based payment · IFRS 3 business
combinations · IFRS 5 held for sale and discontinued operations · IFRS 9 financial
instruments (classification, ECL, derivatives, hedge accounting, modification and
derecognition) · IFRS 10/11/IAS 28 control, joint control, significant influence ·
IFRS 13 fair value · IFRS 15 revenue · IFRS 16 leases · IAS 1 presentation and going
concern · IAS 2 inventories and NRV · IAS 8 policies, estimates and errors · IAS 10
events after the reporting period · IAS 12 deferred tax and the rate reconciliation ·
IAS 16 PPE, revaluation, componentisation · IAS 19 employee benefits and termination
benefits · IAS 21 foreign currency and IFRIC 22 · IAS 23 borrowing costs · IAS 24
related parties · IAS 33 EPS and the JSE Circular on headline earnings · IAS 36
impairment and CGUs · IAS 37 provisions, contingencies, onerous contracts, restructuring,
and IFRIC 1 · IAS 40 investment property · IAS 41 agriculture · IFRS 6 exploration.

## Taxation (South Africa)

Work the provision, not the topic. Requirements → exceptions and exclusions → limitations
→ the effect of applying or not applying it → the year in which it bites → the cash effect.

- **Income Tax Act 58 of 1962**: s11(a) general deduction · s11(d) repairs · s11(e)
  wear and tear · s11(j) doubtful debts · s11(o) scrapping · s12C manufacturing assets
  (and s12C(1)(g) ships) · s13 buildings · s15/s36 mining capital expenditure · s8(4)(a)
  recoupments · s19 and Eighth Schedule para 12A debt benefit on concession or compromise
  · s8F hybrid debt instruments · s8FA hybrid interest · s23M interest limitation · s24J
  interest accrual · s24BA asset-for-share value mismatches · s31 transfer pricing ·
  s40CA · s42–s47 corporate reorganisations · s50B interest withholding · s64E–s64FA
  dividends tax and exemptions · s103(2) assessed loss anti-avoidance · Fourth Schedule
  PAYE · Eighth Schedule CGT, base cost, para 65/66 roll-overs.
- **VAT Act 89 of 1991**: supply, time and value of supply · zero-rating for exports ·
  exempt financial services (s2, s12(a)) · s18 change in use · input tax denials ·
  fringe benefits.
- Other: securities transfer tax (transfers, not issues) · carbon tax · mineral and
  petroleum resources royalty · customs and excise where goods cross borders.
- Always ask: who bears the tax, in which year, and what is the rand amount?

## Audit and assurance

- Acceptance and continuance; independence and the threats in the CPC; fees.
- Planning: understanding the entity, materiality (ISA 320), risk of material
  misstatement at assertion level (ISA 315), significant risks, fraud (ISA 240), laws
  and regulations (ISA 250), related parties (ISA 550), estimates (ISA 540).
- Responses: tests of controls versus substantive procedures; the assertions —
  occurrence, completeness, accuracy, cut-off, classification, existence, rights and
  obligations, valuation, presentation. Say **what** evidence, **how** obtained, and
  **why** it addresses the risk.
- Evidence (ISA 500): sufficiency and appropriateness; external beats internal;
  enquiry alone is never enough; auditor's expert (ISA 620); using internal audit (ISA 610).
- Group audits (ISA 600); service organisations (ISA 402); opening balances (ISA 510).
- Going concern (ISA 570): management assesses, the auditor evaluates; at least twelve
  months from the **end of the reporting period**; material uncertainty adequately
  disclosed → unmodified opinion with a Material Uncertainty section; inadequately
  disclosed → qualified or adverse; basis inappropriate → adverse.
- Reporting (ISA 700/701/705/706): Opinion section first, then Basis. Material but not
  pervasive → qualified; material and pervasive → adverse or disclaimer. Other legal and
  regulatory requirements go in their own section. Reportable irregularities under s45 of
  the Auditing Profession Act. Subsequent events (ISA 560). Written representations (ISA 580).
- Quality: ISQM 1 and ISA 220 — responsibility for the work stays with the engagement
  team, including work produced by tools.

## Financial management

- Valuation: DCF (free cash flow definition, pre- or post-tax consistency, terminal
  value, growth rate), multiples, net asset value, dividend models. Check the discount
  rate matches the cash flows.
- Cost of capital: WACC, CAPM, gearing, the effect of a change in capital structure.
- Investment appraisal: NPV, IRR, payback, real options. Incremental cash flows only;
  exclude financing flows if they are in the discount rate; treat working capital and
  tax correctly; watch inflation consistency.
- Financing: sources, covenants, dilution, rights issues, sale and leaseback, hybrid
  instruments — and the accounting and tax consequence of each.
- Treasury and hedging: FECs, options, swaps; transaction, translation and economic
  exposure; whether hedge accounting is even available.
- Working capital and cash flow forecasting; liquidity and solvency.

## Management decision-making and control

- **Relevant costing**: only future cash flows that *change* as a result of the decision.
  Exclude sunk costs, committed costs, allocated overheads and depreciation. Include
  opportunity costs. Apply every adjustment consistently on both sides of a comparison.
- Limiting factors: contribution per unit of the scarce resource.
- Break-even, margin of safety, operating gearing.
- Make or buy, keep or close, further processing, special orders, product mix.
- Budgeting, variances, transfer pricing.
- Performance measurement: KPIs must be **specific, measurable, controllable by the person
  being measured, aligned to strategy, and balanced as a set** — financial and
  non-financial, short and long term, outcome and leading. Consider gaming, perverse
  incentives, gateways, weightings, malus and clawback, and who verifies the outcome.

## Strategy, risk and governance

- Strategy tools: SWOT, PESTLE, Porter's Five Forces and generic strategies, value chain,
  Ansoff, BCG. Use them to structure thinking, not to display them.
- Risk: identify, assess likelihood and impact, respond (avoid, reduce, transfer, accept),
  monitor. Distinguish strategic, operational, financial, compliance and reputational.
- **King V / King IV**: ethical and effective leadership; organisational ethics;
  responsible corporate citizenship; strategy, performance and reporting; the governing
  body's composition and committees; risk, technology and information governance;
  compliance; remuneration; assurance; stakeholder inclusivity. The six capitals —
  financial, manufactured, intellectual, human, social and relationship, natural — and
  the triple context of economy, society and environment.
- **Companies Act 71 of 2008**: s22 reckless trading · s29/s30 financial statements ·
  s38/s40 issue of shares · s41 shareholder approval for issues to directors and for
  issues exceeding 30% of voting power · s44/s45 financial assistance · s46/s4 solvency
  and liquidity for distributions · s50 securities register · s61 shareholder meetings ·
  s66 board authority · s71 removal of directors · s72(4) and Reg 43 social and ethics
  committee · s75 directors' personal financial interests · s76 directors' duties and the
  business judgement rule · s77 liability · s112/s115 disposal of a greater part of assets
  or undertaking · s129 business rescue.
- **JSE Listings Requirements**: continuing obligations and price-sensitive information,
  related party transactions, specific issues of shares, remuneration policy and
  implementation report voting and the engagement required on significant dissent.
- **&lt;IR&gt; Framework**: the six capitals, value creation over the short, medium and long
  term, the content elements, connectivity, conciseness, reliability and completeness.
- Sustainability: ISSB / IFRS S1 and S2, Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions, greenwashing,
  substantiation and assurance of ESG claims, the UN SDGs.

## Ethics

- **SAICA CPC** fundamental principles: integrity, objectivity, professional competence
  and due care, confidentiality, professional behaviour. Threats: self-interest,
  self-review, advocacy, familiarity, intimidation. Identify → evaluate → address.
- Not being associated with materially false or misleading information, or with
  information that omits or obscures what would make it misleading.
- **NOCLAR** (CPC s260 for accountants in business, s360 in public practice): understand
  and document; raise with management and, if necessary, those charged with governance;
  consider disclosure to an appropriate authority as the last step; confidentiality does
  not override the obligation.
- The ethics triangle: good for self, good for others.
- Structure: what is the dilemma → what bears on it → what should be done, in a
  respectful tone.
