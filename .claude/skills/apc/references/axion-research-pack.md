## The house method

Every summary page in Pack A ends with the same line, and it is the group's answering formula:

> **FACT → WHY IT MATTERS → PRINCIPLE → APPLICATION → CONSEQUENCE → RECOMMENDATION**

Each per-document one-pager is laid out as: **Purpose** · **Confirmed facts and explicit
signals** · **Examiner-focused trigger map** (a four-column table: FACT | WHY IT MATTERS |
PRINCIPLE / APPLICATION | CONSEQUENCE / ACTION) · **Missing information and preparation**
(what evidence is absent, why it is needed, and the template to prepare for it).

Technical triggers are set out as **factor → risk → standard and section → application to
Axion → conclusion and recommendation**.

Two disciplines worth copying: every trigger closes with *information still required before
concluding*; and the packs distinguish what the pre-release **states** from what the group
**infers** ("My reading (not stated in the pre-release)").

## Facts and figures the pack establishes

- **Revenue discrepancy.** Document 2 says R2.4bn FY2026 revenue; Document 5 says R2 620m. The pack flags this and says never to use one silently in a calculation.
- **PIS** well above 9 000 — audit compulsory (Reg 28), social and ethics committee compulsory (s72/Reg 43), but **s94 does not apply** to a private company, so the Audit & Risk Committee is voluntary unless the MOI requires it.
- **King V** effective for financial years beginning on or after 1 January 2026, so Axion's first affected year is **FY2027** (1 July 2026 – 30 June 2027).
- **Cost of capital inputs used:** pre-tax debt 10.5%; cost of equity 18–22%; effective interest rate rose 7.1% → 7.7% across FY2024–FY2026 while policy rates fell.
- **Limiting factor** is contracted MW (and behind it, grid power). Rank customers on **contribution per MW** = capacity fees + power margin + interconnection + ancillary, less the customer's electricity, variable support costs and credit losses — adjusted for ecosystem value, term and credit quality.
- **IFRS 16 lessor split:** "dedicated racks, cages or areas" are physically distinct → an identified asset → contains a lease. "Access to a contracted amount of power capacity" is a capacity right, not physically distinct → IFRS 15. The IFRS 16.15 practical expedient is lessee-only, so **separation is mandatory** and allocation follows IFRS 16.17 → IFRS 15.73–90.
- **Metroloop:** 40% does not create joint control; significant influence is presumed at 20%+ (IAS 28.5). If joint control is established, test legal form → contractual terms → other facts (IFRS 11 B22–B33). Metro fibre vehicles commonly meet B31–B33 (parties take the output and fund the liabilities), which rebuts the joint venture presumption and points to a **joint operation**.

## The four sentences doing the heaviest work

1. "Axion does not provide financing to its customers" (Doc 5) — closes IFRS 15 significant financing *and* the IFRS 18 specified main business activity test.
2. "Customers may contract for capacity before they begin using it" (Doc 5) — stand-ready obligation, and the 5 MW gap.
3. "Purchase commitments may begin before the new facility is fully utilised" (Doc 7) — IAS 37 onerous contract.
4. "Not included in Axion external communications" (Doc 6) — decides the MPM question under IFRS 18.
